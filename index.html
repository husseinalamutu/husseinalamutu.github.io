Engineering Principles

How I Build Platforms & Infrastructure

Reliability and developer velocity are not opposing forces. When systems are designed with high trust, transparency, and self-service guardrails, engineering teams thrive asynchronously.

Infrastructure-as-a-Product

I treat internal software engineers as my primary customers. Rather than forcing developers to submit tickets or learn complex cloud abstractions, I build composable self-service developer platforms with sensible defaults and clear boundaries so product engineers can ship autonomously.

Async-First & Blameless Runbooks

True system reliability is about eliminating single points of failure. In distributed, high-context teams, a production incident isn't resolved until the runbook is documented, postmortems are shared blamelessly, and any on-call engineer can safely reproduce or mitigate the issue without heroics.

AI for Read-Only Toil; Deterministic GitOps for State

AI agents excel at correlating logs, identifying metric anomalies across Datadog/CloudWatch, and summarizing incident timelines. But state mutation—such as terraform apply or IAM grants—must remain deterministic, peer-reviewed, and securely gated through GitOps.

Mission-Driven Platform Reliability

When a platform serves thousands of small business owners and creators whose income depends on their scheduled publishing and analytics, uptime isn't an abstract metric—it directly protects someone's livelihood. Systems must be stable, observable, and built for intentional, long-term health.

Eliminating Manual Ticketing with Infrastructure-as-a-Product

Engineering teams faced friction spinning up new microservices. Provisioning required manual Jira tickets, bespoke Terraform manifests, and customized CI/CD pipelines. This caused configuration drift across environments and stretched new service onboarding time to roughly 5 business days.

Technical Implementation

  • Single Interface: Centralized microservice creation in Backstage, removing cognitive load for product engineers.
  • Parameterization: Modular Terraform blocks parameterized across staging and production to prevent infrastructure drift.
  • Automated Rollbacks: Paired Argo Rollouts with canary releases to achieve zero-downtime deployments with instant rollback on error spikes.

Measurable Impact & Lessons

  • 5 Days → <5 Minutes: New service creation and infrastructure provisioning time dropped by 99%.
  • Zero Manual Tickets: Completely eliminated repetitive platform requests, freeing platform engineers for high-leverage architectural work.
  • Key Insight: Internal platform adoption depends entirely on developer empathy. If an internal tool requires engineers to learn complex cloud abstractions before writing business logic, it will fail.
